# Pilot acceptance roadmap

Advance by evidence gates rather than unsupported timelines/impact percentages.

| Stage | Work | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Controlled exercise | Role permissions, retries, proof/state transitions, fresh captures | Repeatable tests/CI artifacts, physical Android checks, reviewed staff/retention policy |
| One-ward pilot | Authorized rainfall/terrain/drainage; observed incidents/outcomes | Freshness checks, surveyed comparisons, severity-matched baseline/outcome distributions, missed incidents/false alerts/workload |
| City replication | Local datasets, jurisdiction, operating thresholds, closures/resources | Per-city calibration, backup/restore exercise, concurrent load, monitored reliability, FCM device delivery |

Measure report-to-review, dispatch-to-arrival and arrival-to-closure separately. Record dates, sample size, severity, reopenings and unsupported closure attempts. Numerical conservation alone does not establish forecast accuracy.

Jurisdiction enforcement, structured migrations, OS background synchronization, stronger capture validation and surveyed polygon ingestion remain acceptance tasks before expansion.
