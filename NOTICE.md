# Assets and reuse

Original logo, presentation, screenshots and app imagery were supplied by the project team and retained. The owner should confirm upstream rights before redistribution. Design-reference sample identities/counts do not establish operational users/outcomes.

New vectors are authored for this repository. Charts are calculated from committed synthetic inputs; console screenshots show actual local test behavior with synthetic captures. Provenance is documented in `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md`.

Poppins comes from official Google Fonts with SIL Open Font License retained at `mobile/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`. Dependencies retain their upstream licenses; the application displays OpenStreetMap attribution.

The owner has not selected a repository-wide reuse license. Public visibility does not assign code/team/third-party assets a new blanket license.
